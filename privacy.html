Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an event inquiry, request a quote, or contact our catering office, we collect personal information including your full name, email address, phone number, event date, venue location, expected guest count, and dietary/allergy requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to prepare customized catering quotations, coordinate logistics for your event, respond to your inquiries, and fulfill contractual obligations. We never sell, rent, or trade customer information with third parties.

3. Data Security

All client data, booking records, and internal notes are stored securely using industry-standard encryption, password hashing with bcrypt, and secure authentication tokens.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, contact us at events@heritagecatering.com.
