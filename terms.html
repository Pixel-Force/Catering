Terms & Booking Conditions

Last updated: November 2026

1. Inquiry & Quotations

Submitting an inquiry through our website does not constitute a confirmed booking. All official quotations are valid for 14 days from issuance, subject to kitchen and chef availability.

2. Deposit & Payment Terms

A non-refundable 30% deposit is required upon quotation acceptance to secure the event date and schedule the culinary team. The remaining balance (70%) is due 7 business days prior to the event date.

3. Cancellation & Rescheduling

Cancellations made 14 or more calendar days prior to the event date receive a full refund or complimentary date transfer. Cancellations between 7 and 13 days forfeit the initial deposit to cover committed fresh ingredient sourcing.

4. Guest Count Guarantees

Final guaranteed guest numbers and dietary restrictions must be confirmed no later than 5 business days prior to the event. Increases may be accommodated based on ingredient availability.
