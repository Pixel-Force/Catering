Flawless Outdoor Gastronomy

Outdoor catering presents unique logistical challenges: temperature control, food protection, power access, and shifting weather. Grand Heritage operates self-contained mobile kitchen units equipped with commercial refrigeration, insulated warming cambros, and silent generator power to guarantee your food is served at peak restaurant temperature.

From live Mediterranean lamb chop grilling and whole spit-roasted suckling pig to chilled oyster raw bars and fresh summer fruit spritzers, our outdoor menus bring lively theatrical energy to your event.
