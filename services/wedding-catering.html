Luxury Wedding Catering

From sunset cocktail hors d'oeuvres to decadent multi-course seated dinners and midnight cravings, we turn your wedding reception into a culinary masterpiece.

Your Wedding, Perfectly Catered

We believe no two couples share the same love story, and no two wedding menus should be identical. Whether your dream reception is a formal black-tie ballroom gala in Toronto, a romantic vineyard celebration in Niagara, or an intimate lakeside tent reception, our culinary team designs a customized menu reflecting your heritage, taste preferences, and favorite dishes.

With a dedicated Banquet Captain assigned to each wedding, you and your partner will enjoy a stress-free celebration knowing that every course will be plated hot, synchronized, and presented with Michelin-level finesse.

The Wedding Culinary Journey

1
Cocktail Hour & Grazing Reception

Passed gourmet canapés (Truffle Arancini, Smoked Salmon Blinis, Wagyu Crostini) accompanied by artisan cheese boards and signature wedding welcome spritzers.

2
Plated or Luxury Buffet Dinner

Seated service featuring dual entrees: 12-hour braised short ribs paired with pan-seared Chilean sea bass, baby carrots, and saffron risotto, complete with specialized vegetarian and Halal dishes.

3
Dessert Table & Midnight Fuel

Handcrafted French macaron towers, Belgian chocolate ganache tarts, and late-night savory stations like hot gourmet poutine or artisan beef sliders for dancing guests.
